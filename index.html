Which weekend?

28-29 November still wins, and with the Düsseldorf-then-Cologne plan it's the only November weekend that works for both days. Open the doors to see why.

Skip it

No Christmas markets open in either city.

Too early

Düsseldorf's markets only open on 19 Nov, and Cologne's Dom market on 16 Nov.

Sunday is lost

Saturday in Düsseldorf works, but Sunday 22 Nov is Totensonntag: Düsseldorf's markets are shut all day and Cologne's until 18:00. No Heinzels ice rink yet either.

Our pick
Go now

Saturday: every Düsseldorf market open until 21:00. Sunday: Cologne's Dom market from 11:00 and Heinzels with its ice rink. First Advent, so busy.

Tips: book now, bring cash (Düsseldorf's market FAQ says there are no cash machines on the markets), and aim to be at Cologne's Dom market close to its 11:00 opening on Sunday, before the Advent crowds.

Sample plan

Park once in Düsseldorf and walk or take the U-Bahn all Saturday. Sunday, drive 40 to 50 minutes to Cologne, do the markets, and head home from there.

Saturday Düsseldorf

  1. 07:30Leave Rotterdam, about 2 to 2.5 hours. Park at the stay or a garage, drop bags if the host allows.
  2. 10:30-13:00Königsallee, Kö-Bogen and Schadowstraße: the fashion stretch for Kiara, Liz and Mike, Libeskind architecture for Giamo.
  3. 13:00-14:30Altbier lunch in the Altstadt: Uerige, Füchschen or Schumacher.
  4. 14:30-17:30Markets: Rathaus, Burgplatz Ferris wheel at dusk, Roncalli promenade on the Rhine. Skating at Corneliusplatz, or Mike's arcade detour.
  5. 17:30-19:00Check in and get ready.
  6. 19:00-21:30Dinner: a starred restaurant, or ramen in Little Tokyo.
  7. 21:30-23:30Cocktails: The Paris Club rooftop or Square Bar.
  8. 23:30 onwardsGlüh Dich Glücklich at the Kasematten, then THEGATE or Cube.

Sunday Cologne

  1. 09:30-10:30Brunch and check out.
  2. 10:30Drive to Cologne, about 40 km. Park in Deutz (Lanxess Arena) and walk over the Hohenzollern Bridge for the Dom view, or use a central garage.
  3. 11:30-13:00Dom market, just after the 11:00 opening.
  4. 13:00-14:00Kölsch and lunch at Früh am Dom. Compare it with yesterday's Altbier.
  5. 14:00-16:30Heinzels: skating or ice curling, Glühwein, bratwurst. Liz can split off to a cat café if one is open.
  6. 16:30Drive home, about 260 km and 2.5 to 3 hours.

Driving and parking

Rotterdam to Düsseldorf is about 230 km and 2 to 2.5 hours. Düsseldorf to Cologne is about 40 km and 40 to 50 minutes. Cologne back to Rotterdam is about 260 km and 2.5 to 3 hours.

Green sticker required in both cities. Düsseldorf and Cologne both have low-emission zones that need a green Umweltplakette, Dutch plates included. The fine is €100. Order one online or from TÜV/Dekra and allow a few days for delivery.

Parking

  • Düsseldorf, Saturday: at the stay if it has a spot, otherwise a garage such as Contipark Altstadt Rheinufer, or the free P+R Südfriedhof with the U72 into town. (The free Provinzial garage deal is only on 5, 12 and 19 December.)
  • Cologne, Sunday: the Dom, Hauptbahnhof, Philharmonie and Groß St. Martin garages sit right by the markets (koeln.de) but fill up on Advent weekends. The city suggests the edge of the centre or the Lanxess Arena in Deutz (Stadt Köln), which gives you the bridge walk too.

Pick Sunday's driver before Saturday night: the limit is 0.5 per mille in Germany (0.0 for new drivers), and alcohol from a late night can still count the next morning.
